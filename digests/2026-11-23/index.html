Capture projects · 1/11

Scotland consents the 910 MW Peterhead carbon capture power station

  • Scottish Ministers granted Section 36 consent for SSE Thermal and Equinor's 910 MW gas plant with capture.
  • Designed to capture at least 90% of flue gas, up to 1.5 million tonnes of CO2 per year.
  • It anchors Acorn CCS, which aims for up to 20 million tonnes per year at peak across the Scottish Cluster.
  • Why it matters: investment still depends on the wider transport and storage network being delivered.

Source: Carbon Herald, 17 Nov 2026

Policy · 2/11

EU and UK conclude talks to link their carbon markets

  • Announced at the EU-UK Summit in Brussels; both sides aim for it to take effect in 2028, subject to legal procedures.
  • Mutual recognition of allowances plus CBAM exemptions for goods traded between the two.
  • CCSA estimates average savings of about 11 euros per tonne in storage costs, up to 16 euros for EU emitters.
  • Why it matters: a connected storage market could open UK storage to EU emitters.

Source: Carbon Herald, 20 Nov 2026

Policy · 3/11

COP31 draft drops fossil phase-out wording and elevates CCUS

  • Revised Antalya Accord draft (19 Nov) removes commitments to move away from coal, oil and gas.
  • Proposes a provisional target of at least 500 million tonnes of CO2 captured per year by 2035.
  • Longer-term trajectory of 1.3 to 2.5 billion tonnes per year, versus about 38 billion tonnes of energy CO2 emissions today.
  • Why it matters: strong opposition, so the final text was still open when reported.

Source: Carbon Herald, 20 Nov 2026

Transport and storage · 4/11

CO2next completes FEED for Rotterdam liquid CO2 terminal

  • Gasunie, Vopak, Shell and TotalEnergies' venture finished front-end engineering design.
  • Phase 1: two jetties, four storage tanks; 5.4 Mtpa initially, scalable to 10 Mtpa.
  • Tanks buffer ship arrivals into a steady stream feeding the Aramis offshore system.
  • Why it matters: next steps are procurement tendering and a final investment decision.

Source: Carbon Herald, 19 Nov 2026

Funding and economics · 5/11

IEEFA: Alberta CCS economics weak, taxpayer risk rising

  • IEEFA update (19 Nov) says Alberta CCS relies on emission performance credits and public support.
  • Lower carbon-price ceilings, credit oversupply and weak compliance demand erode cash flow.
  • Warns of higher cost for credit price floors and carbon contracts for difference.
  • Recommends support tied to verified, durable capture and storage, not upfront subsidies.

Source: Carbon Herald (IEEFA analysis), 20 Nov 2026

Business and solvents · 6/11

Honeywell solvent capture selected for Phelan Green eSAF complex

  • Phelan eFuels contracted Honeywell to license its Advanced Solvent Carbon Capture technology (announced 18 Nov).
  • Saldanha Bay, South Africa: captured biogenic CO2 plus green hydrogen make aviation fuel.
  • Capacity 140,000 tonnes per year over three phases, starting with 35,000 tonnes in Phase 1.
  • Why it matters: solvent capture integrated into a CO2-to-fuel chain.

Source: Carbon Herald, 20 Nov 2026

Technology and research · 7/11

Oregon State's BVR-X MOF captures CO2 from humid gas

  • New metal-organic framework keeps water and CO2 in separate regions of its pores.
  • Lab tests: efficient capture from a highly humid stream with only 4% CO2; dozens of capture-release cycles.
  • Published in Angewandte Chemie; patent application filed.
  • Why it matters: water vapour is a standing problem for sorbent capture of flue gas.

Source: ScienceDaily (Oregon State University), 16 Nov 2026

DAC · 8/11

Japan's Planet Savers raises over $10M for zeolite DAC

  • Pre-Series A of JPY 1.08 billion (about $7.2M) led by ANRI.
  • Plus government and Tokyo grants above JPY 1.6 billion (about $10.7M).
  • Zeolite adsorbent with an electrically driven system, field-tested in Japan.
  • Plans a storage-based removal project in South Australia with Carbon Vault.

Source: Carbon Herald, 20 Nov 2026

Markets · 9/11

Canada sets up standing offers for permanent carbon removal credits

  • On 19 Nov Canada listed 19 domestic projects as qualified suppliers for federal purchases to 31 March 2029.
  • Technologies include DAC, BECCS, biochar and mineralisation.
  • Described as the first national government to set competitive standing offers for permanent CDR.
  • Why it matters: government demand supports project financing.

Source: Carbon Herald, 20 Nov 2026

Utilisation · 10/11

Germany launches 24 million euro DISCO2 CO2 utilisation programme

  • BMFTR funds a three-year initiative: 41 partners, 13 R&D projects.
  • Covers catalytic, plasma and electrochemical routes to ethylene, acetate, methanol and syngas.
  • Includes life cycle assessments and a legal workstream on regulatory barriers.
  • Kick-off conference held 10-11 Nov.

Source: Carbon Herald, 17 Nov 2026

Curiosity · 11/11

Mazda's onboard capture: 804 g of CO2 in race test

  • Upgraded system captured 804 g in the Fuji 24-Hour race (June 2026), versus 84 g in Nov 2025.
  • Public road trials planned for 2028; commercial target 2035.
  • Aims to capture 20-40% of exhaust CO2; prototype adds about 50 kg.
  • Why it matters: mobile capture is niche but shows adsorption-desorption scaling.

Source: Carbon Herald, 19 Nov 2026
